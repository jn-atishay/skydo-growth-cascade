The cascade · spend → revenue

Twelve levers, top to bottom

Each layer's gain feeds the next. Numbers below run on your confirmed monthly funnel (Mar): ₹123.6L paid → ≈28.8k leads → 13,330 MQL → 2,684 ONB → 961 activations.

Layer 0 · Media
Spend & impressions
₹123.6L/mo paid · cheaper CPM
1 · Lower CPM. App-install inventory + owned in-app surfaces add cheap/free impressions vs CPC-inflated competitor-keyword search. Cost per impression ↓.
2 · Lower CPC / CPL → more leads, same spend. Cheaper impressions flow straight to cheaper leads. At blended CPL ~₹634, a 10% CPL drop = +11% leads (~28.8k → ~31.9k) for the same ₹123.6L.
3 · A new acquisition rail. Google App Campaigns, Meta App Promotion, Apple Search Ads (CPI ₹40–90) diversify off search you're losing 80–90% impression share on.
4 · Cleaner MMP signal → lower CPA everywhere. Deterministic activation events back to Google/Meta fix what your GTM/Core pixel can't — lower CPA across all paid spend, not just the app.
▼
Layer 1 · Top funnel
Leads → Signups
≈28.8k → 31.9k leads
5 · Mobile-native signup. 72% of your traffic is already mobile; apps convert 2–3× mobile web. Google/Apple sign-in + autofill lift click→signup. More of the extra leads survive to signup.
▼
Layer 2 · Activation I
Signup → KYC → ONB
2,684 → ~3,600 ONB/mo
6 · Plug the KYC leak. 20–30% abandon onboarding on KYC. Mobile DigiLocker/Aadhaar/camera + resume-push recover them. Lead→ONB 9.3% → ~11.5%.
7 · Scale ONB without scaling sales. Self-serve routes the SMB long-tail around your weakest stage (MQL→Deal 14.5%). More direct ONBs, lower cost-per-ONB.
▼
Layer 3 · Activation II
ONB → Activation
961 → ~1,476 acts/mo
8 · Money-landed push + faster first collection. The highest-intent moment, delivered instantly; nudges drive the first transaction. ONB→Act 35.8% → ~41%.
▼
Layer 4 · Retention
Repeat & frequency
↑ LTV · more repeat collections
9 · Home-screen presence + settlement push. Keeps a low-frequency product top-of-mind between transactions → more repeat collections, lower churn.
10 · Consumption turns a tool into a habit. Skydo Live, daily FX brief, community — the LinkedIn-games playbook (84% next-day return). Weekly active, not just transactional.
▼
Layer 5 · Expansion
Revenue per customer
↑ ARPU · wallet share
11 · Cross-sell Imports + Cards at zero marginal CAC. The financial-OS front door — offer payouts & card payments at the moment money lands. Revenue expansion with no new acquisition.
12 · Free owned channel. Push replaces ~₹1/msg WhatsApp (₹13–15L/yr per weekly touch) — OpEx saved, redeployed to spend, restarting the cascade.
